# Cartoon asset provenance

All three assets were generated with the built-in Imagegen tool, inspected, and copied into the project. White backgrounds were requested. No CLI/API fallback was used.

## one-step.png

Saved at `pub_src/site/static/images/one-step.png`.

Prompt: Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: interior cartoon for an original 1986 programming magazine course. Draw one wide black-and-white graphite pencil cartoon, clean white paper background, no border, no text and no lettering. A bespectacled hobbyist at a 1980s desktop IBM XT compatible with monochrome CRT and floppy drive is very carefully carrying a single oversized wooden stair tread to a tiny staircase beside the desk. Warm dry visual humor about building software one small step at a time. Hand-drawn pencil line, restrained crosshatching, period magazine spot illustration. Landscape composition with plenty of white margins. Original characters, no logos, no modern devices.

## inspect-bytes.png

Saved at `pub_src/site/static/images/inspect-bytes.png`.

Prompt: Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original interior pencil cartoon for a black-and-white 1986 programming magazine. Single wide spot illustration on clean white paper, no text, no lettering, no border. A meticulous woman programmer in rolled sleeves, seated at a wooden workbench, uses an enormous magnifying glass to inspect three tiny plain square tiles laid in a neat row; a patient cat peers through the other side of the lens with a comically enlarged eye. A small 1980s floppy disk and a pencil sit on the bench. Quiet dry humor about inspecting every byte. Hand-drawn graphite pencil contours and light crosshatching, restrained magazine illustration, lots of whitespace, original character, no modern equipment or logos.

## two-passes.png

Saved at `pub_src/site/static/images/two-passes.png`.

Prompt: Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original black-and-white graphite pencil spot cartoon for an independent 1986 programming magazine. Wide composition, clean white paper, no text, no letters, no symbols, no border. A fussy mustached librarian wearing a cardigan stands beside a wooden shelf with a notebook, carefully counting the empty spaces between a row of books. A small wheeled cart behind him is piled with books waiting to be shelved; he has clearly decided to count places before moving books. Dry visual humor about doing a job in two passes. Crisp handmade pencil line, spare crosshatching, restrained period editorial cartoon, original character, generous whitespace, no modern objects or branding.
