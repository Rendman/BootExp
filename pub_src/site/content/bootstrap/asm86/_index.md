This collection will build an assembler in the assembly language accepted by BOOTASM. Its first executable will be made with BOOTASM; its construction will then proceed in small steps toward assembling its own source.

The BOOTASM collection supplies the required BASIC foundation and first assembler. ASM86 construction has not begun.
